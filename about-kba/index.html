About Kokusai Business Advisory

Kokusai Business Advisory is an independent Australia-Japan advisory firm bringing together senior Australian and Japanese advisers to provide strategic counsel to organisations operating across both countries.

Our story

Why KBA was established

2017KBA founded by its Principals

KBA was founded in 2017 by its Principals, all “old Japan Hands” who had known each other for decades through their involvement in promoting Australia–Japan relations. Its establishment followed a major Japanese investment in Australia that encountered significant difficulties, particularly with governance and post-merger integration.

That investment demonstrated how commercial ambition can be undermined when governance arrangements, management expectations and the realities of operating across two business environments are not sufficiently understood.

The Principals decided to establish KBA to help organisations identify and address these issues earlier, so that similar outcomes do not discourage future Australia-Japan investment and cooperation.

Since then, KBA has seen comparable challenges arise across Japanese investments in Australia, from local management and business practices to regulatory complexity and changes in expatriate leadership.

The Principals came to know each other over many years through their work across Australian and Japanese business, government and community organisations. KBA brought together relationships, and a way of working, that had already proved their value.

Why Australia and Japan

KBA’s Principals regard the Australia-Japan relationship as the most important in the Asia-Pacific region, with long-term potential across commerce, technology, geostrategy and food and energy security. Their conviction reflects the depth of the bilateral relationship, built on decades of commercial exchange, institutional cooperation and mutual trust.

For KBA’s Principals, this relationship is also personal. Over decades, they have developed deep business and social ties with Japan, including friendships that extend well beyond their professional relationships.

The people who shape KBA

KBA is defined by the individuals who make up the firm. Each Principal brings their own experience, judgement and perspective, contributing to an advisory firm shaped by its people rather than a standard consulting model.
