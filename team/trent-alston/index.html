About Trent

Experience shaped by capital, partnerships and cross-border platforms

Trent Alston combines commercial expertise with an ability to forge capital partnerships, work across cultures and interpret complex data for commercial advantage.

That experience was built as an executive at Lendlease, Colonial First State Global Asset Management and Challenger Limited. More recently, Trent has taken on a non-executive career, with directorships at ASX-listed Abacus Property Group and Landcom, the New South Wales Government’s state-owned corporation. He also works as a retained adviser to several groups.

In both markets, the best partnerships start with understanding what each side needs from the other over the long term.
