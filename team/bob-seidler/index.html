About Bob

Experience shaped by law, boardrooms and government

Bob Seidler AM is a lawyer, company director and senior Australia–Japan business leader whose career has spanned more than five decades, including senior roles in both Australia and Japan.

His perspective has been shaped from several sides of cross-border decision-making: as legal adviser, board director, business leader and government representative.

The relationships that matter most between Australia and Japan are built long before there is a transaction to discuss.

Advisory perspective

What Bob brings to clients

A board-level view of cross-border issues

Bob has held board positions in Australian and Japanese companies, including Mitsubishi Bank of Australia, CIMIC Group and Hitachi Australia. This experience provides first-hand understanding of different perspectives and how decisions are made in both Australia and Japan.

Governance & Advisory Board Services

First-hand experience of market entry

For over 50 years, Bob has advised on market entry into both Australia and Japan, often initiating proposals and bringing them to fruition.

Market Entry & Opportunity Assessment

Major investment decisions, from legal counsel to the boardroom

More than 50 years in international law placed Bob alongside major investment and financing decisions across banking, real estate, resources and infrastructure.

His overlapping board and government career provides him with a unique perspective on such decisions and opportunities.

Acquisitions, Investments & Partnerships

Relationships across business and government

Bob has a remarkable network of relationships in Australia and Japan across business and government, built over decades of involvement in Australia–Japan business and government. They are relationships built through long-term trust, not individual transactions.
