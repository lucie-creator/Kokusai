KBA’s perspective on market entry

KBA brings experience and judgement to the decisions that shape market entry: whether an opportunity is right for the organisation, how to enter and who to enter with.

Market assessment · Commercial viability · Strategic fit

The opportunity in the context of the organisation

KBA brings an independent strategic perspective to market entry opportunities, drawing on its knowledge of Australian and Japanese business. Its experience and relationships may surface opportunities not yet considered, challenge existing assumptions and influence how, or whether, an organisation proceeds.

02

Market entry strategy · Entry options · Route to market

Navigating market entry options

Entering a new market involves many different considerations, ranging from assessing the market, finding a suitable partner and assessing the form of entry, to legal, financial and regulatory approval issues.

Where KBA does not have the capability to assist in any of these areas, KBA will assist clients in engaging the appropriate specialists to do so.

03

Partner selection · Strategic fit · Cross-border partnerships

Compatibility beyond credentials

A successful investment depends on more than a strong business case. The right partner can strengthen that business case and contribute to its long-term success.

KBA looks beyond capability and reputation to leadership compatibility, communication and cultural fit, identifying partners equipped to work effectively with the organisation over time.

04

Local market context · Decision-making · Operating realities

What changes the decision locally

KBA’s Australian and Japanese Principals know where differences in governance, decision-making, business practices and stakeholder relationships change how an opportunity should be pursued and where assumptions from one market can mislead in the other.

Where market entry leads to an acquisition, investment or joint venture, KBA can assist beyond partner selection, through the conclusion of the transaction and into post-transaction implementation, where cross-cultural issues and communication become critical.
