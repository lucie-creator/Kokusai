How KBA works

Advisory Partnership

Scroll

Independent advice, backed by the collective experience and judgement of KBA’s Principals.

KBA works as an advisory partner to Australian and Japanese organisations on commercial decisions, investments and established business relationships. Its model brings its Principals together as a whole-of-team approach to a transaction, combining their experience and established relationships through a single client relationship.

KBA can also act as an Advisory Board on an ongoing basis.

One relationship, the whole team

Each engagement has a lead Principal who maintains the client relationship and draws on the experience and connections of the wider team.

Clients benefit from the collective judgement of KBA’s Principals, working together rather than as individual advisers. Different Principals contribute to discussions and decisions where their particular experience is relevant, bringing perspectives developed across business, investment, governance and government in both countries.

The principles behind the advice

Independent judgement

KBA’s advice is not contingent on a transaction proceeding. Its Principals may question the assumptions behind an investment, challenge its timing or structure, or recommend against proceeding when it doesn’t make sense to proceed or the risks outweigh the potential benefits.

Their judgement extends beyond the immediate business case to the governance, management, organisational and cultural arrangements that will determine how an investment or partnership operates in practice.

Integrity & discretion

Before accepting an engagement, KBA considers potential conflicts with existing clients, including whether it already advises a competitor in the prospective client’s sector. Its commitment not to advise competing organisations protects the value of the relationships and knowledge available to each client.

Discussions with KBA remain confidential. KBA never discloses who it works with.

When KBA becomes involved

KBA encourages organisations to engage its Principals early, before key commercial decisions are made or specialist advisers are appointed. This allows KBA to contribute to the initial assessment of an opportunity, question underlying assumptions and identify the expertise required.

KBA works alongside legal, tax and financial advisers, complementing their specialist advice. Getting KBA’s advice in the selection of key advisers will assist in getting the best advisers who understand working in the bilateral relationship.

An ongoing advisory relationship

KBA’s involvement may centre on a single issue or transaction, or on an ongoing advisory relationship.

  1. 01

    Through an ongoing advisory relationship, KBA’s Principals develop a detailed understanding of the organisation, its people and priorities. This enables KBA to assist in building the business and its network, as well as addressing day-to-day issues.

  2. 02

    This continuity allows KBA to carry that knowledge across changes in senior management, preserving the context of established investments, partnerships and commercial relationships.

  3. 03

    KBA’s Principals also facilitate communication between local management and head office, particularly on matters that are difficult to address across organisational boundaries.
