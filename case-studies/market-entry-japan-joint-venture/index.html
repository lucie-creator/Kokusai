Case studies

Establishing a joint venture in Japan

KBA identified an opportunity in Japan for an Australian financial institution and helped turn it into a new joint venture with the right Japanese partner.

Market entryFinancial services

Client
Australian financial institution
Business situation
Establishing a business in Japan
Expertise
Joint venture & strategic partnership
Industry
Financial services

01The situation

An expansion plan without Japan in it

The client was considering international expansion but had not identified Japan as a target market.

02Why KBA

KBA identified Japan as a strategic growth opportunity

Through an established relationship with senior management, KBA was able to bring the opportunity directly to the client, setting out the commercial case for entering the Japanese market.

KBA was subsequently retained to advise on the market entry strategy, including identifying the right Japanese partner.

03KBA’s approach

Partner selection, engagement and negotiation

  1. Defining the partner requirements

    KBA established the criteria for a suitable partner, including relevant sector experience, English-language communication and cultural fit with the Australian organisation.

  2. Identifying and opening the relationship

    KBA identified a suitable Japanese organisation, approached the prospective partner and secured agreement to proceed with introductory meetings.

  3. Advising through negotiations

    KBA advised the Australian organisation throughout negotiations, providing strategic, business and cross-cultural advice as the parties worked towards establishing the joint venture.

04The outcome

New joint venture in Japan and an ongoing advisory relationship

An Australian financial institution now operates a joint venture in a market it had not considered. KBA’s advice continued once the business was running, resolving misunderstandings and helping build new relationships as it grew.
