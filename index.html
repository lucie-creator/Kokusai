How KBA advises

Whether exploring an opportunity, entering a market, building a partnership or managing an established operation, clients gain the greatest value by engaging KBA at the outset, before plans take shape or other advisers are appointed.

Cross-cultural understanding

Because KBA, with its Principals based in both Australia and Japan, has been involved in the bilateral relationship for many decades, KBA understands how expectations, decision-making and business practices differ between Australia and Japan, helping clients with market entry, to establish relationships, develop growth strategies, select advisers and generally to anticipate misunderstandings before they affect decisions or relationships.

Cross-cultural business alignment

Judgement before outcome

KBA’s advice does not depend on a transaction proceeding. Its Principals challenge assumptions, timing and structure and will advise against proceeding when the case does not stand up to scrutiny.

One advisory relationship

Clients engage KBA as a team. Its Principals contribute their collective expertise and relationships to assist their clients.

Integrity and discretion

KBA is selective about the organisations it advises, generally acting for only one client in an industry, ensuring there are no conflicts of interest and preserving the confidentiality of its advice.
